与岁月书斋编辑部合作，写给 60 后、70 后的读书人。每本书由主编审校，做成约 45 分钟的讲解，分 5 到 8 节慢慢听；听到哪一节，就可以问哪一节。讲解里的人物和年代让你想起什么，也可以说给书房听，经你确认后记成一张记忆卡，慢慢攒成自己的回忆录。Made with our editorial partner for readers born in the 1960s and 70s. Each book is an editor-reviewed lecture of about 45 minutes in five to eight sections, and you can ask about whichever section you are on. When a person or a year in the lecture brings something back, tell the study; with your say-so it becomes a memory card, and in time, your own memoir.

合作项目Partnership

医疗研究软件 · 与 Oslian LLC 合作Clinical research software · with Oslian LLC

Oslian 做的是临床预测方向的研究软件：读入 FHIR 格式的病历数据，预测病人全身各项指标接下来的走势，并且只说证据支持的部分。它包含两套彼此隔离的系统：Oslian builds research software for clinical forecasting: it reads FHIR records, forecasts a patient's whole-body trajectory, and never claims more than the evidence supports. It has two systems kept deliberately apart:

监测Monitoring

把数据流画在人体图上，同一器官的信号放在一起。模型预测和沿用上次的数值分开标注，每个数都能追到原始记录。Signals drawn on an anatomical figure, grouped by organ. Model forecasts and carried-forward values are labelled apart, and every value traces back to its record.

用药安全Medication safety

用确定的规则给候选药物排序，每一步理由都画出来，安全规则对照药品说明书原句检查。A deterministic engine ranks drug candidates and draws the reasoning behind each step; safety rules are checked against label text.

仅供研究使用，不是医疗器械。只用合成数据，不接入任何医院系统；不做诊断、不给治疗建议、不发临床警报，也不会自动开药。Research use only; not a medical device. Synthetic data only, with no connection to any hospital system. No diagnosis, no treatment recommendation, no clinical alert, no automated prescribing.
