Technology · what's actually running技术 · 已在线上运行

Not a system prompt — real retrieval, persistent memory, and grounding you can trace.不是一段 system prompt——而是真实的检索、持久的记忆，和每一句都有出处的回答。

01

Knowledge-graph RAG知识图谱检索

Every book gets its own knowledge graph. Answers walk the graph, pull the exact passage, and cite it — click the citation and the reader jumps to the line.每本书都有自己的知识图谱。回答沿着图谱检索，取回原文段落，并附上出处——点一下出处，阅读器就翻到那一行。

graph-RAG · clickable citations · live in Glossagraph-RAG · 可点出处 · Glossa 已上线
02

Memory that lasts — and fades会记，也会忘

Three tiers — permanent, long-term, short-term — so what matters is kept and trivia decays on its own, with semantic recall over a vector store.记忆分三层：永久、长期、短期。要紧的留下来，琐碎的自己淡去；需要时，按语义找回。

Qdrant · text-embedding-3-small · time-decayQdrant 向量召回 · 分层 · 时间衰减
03

Canon grounding正典打底

Characters grow out of real source material — fan wikis, Wikipedia — with a keyword lorebook firing within a token budget. Told, above all else, not to invent.角色从真实材料里长出来：粉丝百科、维基百科打底，词条库按关键词触发，控制在 token 预算之内。第一条规矩：宁可不答，也不编造。

Vision · where this goes愿景 · 更远的地方

Our bet isn't smarter answers. It's someone who remembers — and stays.我们押注的不是更聪明的答案，而是一个记得你的人——而且一直都在。

Remembers记得

Memory that crosses sessions and years — what matters is kept, the trivial fades. Like a person.记忆跨越会话与岁月——要紧的留住，琐碎的淡去。像人一样。

Stays常在

Not a tool waiting to be opened — a presence that writes first, speaks aloud, and notices when you've been away too long.不是一个等你打开的工具，而是常在身边的陪伴——会先来信，会开口说话，你久未出现，它会记挂。

Grounded有据

Every sentence traces back to something real — a passage, a memory, a source. Silence over invention.每一句话都能追溯到真实的来处——一段原文、一条记忆、一个出处。宁可不说，也不杜撰。

It starts with two companions — one for your life, one for your books. Then everyone who needs to be remembered: a trainee, a student, a patient, a reader growing old with their shelves.从两个产品开始：一个陪你生活，一个陪你读书。往后，我们想陪的，是每一个想被记住的人——培训中的学员、课堂上的学生、诊室里的病人，还有和书架一起变老的读书人。
