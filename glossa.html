打开一本书，有五个页面Five pages for every book

讲解Lecture每本书约 45 分钟，分 5 到 8 节，由主编审校。可以调速、跳节、看讲稿，下次从停下的地方接着听。About 45 minutes a book in five to eight sections, reviewed by an editor. Change speed, skip sections, read the script, and pick up where you stopped.
脉络Outline这本书讲了哪几部分、每部分讲什么。书里人物和事件的关系图也在这里。What the book covers and how its parts fit, plus a map of its people and events.
问书Ask听到哪一节，就问哪一节，打字说话都行。回答会注明依据的是讲解里哪一段。Ask about the section you are on, typed or spoken. Answers say which part of the lecture they rest on.
笔记Notes摘下喜欢的句子，写几句自己的想法，都按书收在一起。Keep the lines you love and your own thoughts, gathered by book.
记忆Memories聊天时说起的往事，经你确认后用你自己的话记成一张卡片。存不存、给不给家人看，都由你决定。Stories you tell become cards in your own words, once you confirm them. Whether to keep or share them with family is always your call.

氛围Ambience

可以换一幅莫奈、一张星空做背景，再开一点小雨或篝火声。讲解响起时，环境声会自动调小。字号、字重、纸色和深浅色也都能调。Put a Monet or a night sky behind the page and add a little rain or a fire. When the lecture plays, the sounds step back. Text size, weight, paper colour and dark mode are all yours to set.

慢慢写成一本回忆录Slowly, a memoir

讲解里的一个人、一个年份，常常会让人想起自己的事。书房会温和地问一句，你愿意说就说；确认过的记忆卡一张张攒起来，先汇成月度小册，再慢慢整理成一本属于你的回忆录。这通常要一年以上。A person or a year in a lecture often brings back something of your own. The study asks gently, and you say as much as you like. Confirmed memory cards gather into a monthly booklet, and over a year or more, into a memoir that is yours.
